# Source Basis

Use this file when a user asks why a rule exists, wants primary-source justification, or
needs to audit the `nature-cover` skill against real submission guidance.

## Source map

| Skill rule | Primary support |
|---|---|
| A cover letter accompanies the submission and addresses the editor. | Nature Portfolio submission guidance asks authors to include a cover letter to the editor with the manuscript submission. |
| The letter should explain the advance and why the work is significant and timely. | Springer Nature / Nature author guidance advises the cover letter to state the key findings, their significance, and why the work suits the journal. |
| The letter should argue fit to the specific journal's scope and readership. | Nature Portfolio journals select on scope and breadth of interest; author guidance advises explaining why the manuscript is appropriate for the journal. |
| Editors triage and may reject without review on scope/significance grounds. | Nature editorial process descriptions state that editors assess suitability and may decline manuscripts without external review. |
| Authors may suggest and oppose reviewers. | Nature Portfolio submission policies allow authors to suggest preferred reviewers and to request the exclusion of specific reviewers. |
| Submissions must be original and not under consideration elsewhere. | Nature Portfolio editorial policies require that submitted work is not under consideration or published elsewhere (no dual submission). |
| Competing interests must be declared. | Nature Portfolio competing-interests policy requires authors to declare any competing financial or non-financial interests. |
| Ethics approvals and consent must be in place and disclosed for relevant studies. | Nature Portfolio ethics policies require appropriate approvals and informed consent for human/animal research, stated in the manuscript. |
| Presubmission enquiries are available to test scope before full submission. | Several Nature Portfolio journals offer a presubmission enquiry mechanism for authors to ask editors about suitability. |
| Manuscript transfer between Nature Portfolio journals is supported. | Nature Portfolio operates a manuscript transfer/cascade service between journals. |
