# Chinese-author alignment

Open this when the user writes in Chinese, provides Chinese submission notes, or needs
bilingual cover-letter wording. Pairs with `static/core/chinese-mode.md`.

## Common CN-to-EN cover-letter failure modes

- **背景开头 (background-first opening).** Chinese drafts often open with "随着……的快速发展"
  and reach the advance only in paragraph two. A Nature editor wants the advance and hook
  first. Restructure so the science leads.
- **过度宣称 (overclaiming).** "重大突破", "首次", "填补国际空白" translated literally become
  red flags. Convert to calibrated, evidence-backed claims, or cut. A bare "first" invites
  a reviewer to disprove it.
- **泛泛的期刊契合 (generic journal fit).** "贵刊影响力大、读者广泛" is not a fit argument.
  Replace with a journal-specific scope match (see `references/fit-and-significance.md`).
- **信当成第二个摘要 (letter as second abstract).** Listing every result and method. The
  letter argues; it does not summarise. Keep the single headline result.
- **声明缺失或代填 (missing or assumed declarations).** Chinese drafts may omit competing
  interests / author agreement, or the author may not have stated them. Never default to
  "none"; flag as author-input.
- **过长 (over length).** Translated-and-expanded letters drift past one page. Enforce the
  ~250–500-word budget.

## Bilingual intake questions

When the Chinese input lacks what a strong letter needs, ask (in Chinese) for:

1. 目标期刊是哪一本？（契合论证必须针对具体期刊，不能写通用版）
2. 这篇论文最核心的一句话贡献是什么？最强的一个关键结果/数据是什么？
3. 这项工作解决了此前哪个尚未解决的问题？
4. 信件类型：正式投稿信、预投稿咨询，还是转投/再投？
5. 有无利益冲突？全体作者是否已同意投稿？涉及人/动物实验的伦理审批是否就绪？
6. 是否需要推荐审稿人或回避审稿人？如有，请提供姓名与理由（我不会代为编造）。
